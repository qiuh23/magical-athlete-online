# Bounded local stadium shadows

The previous renderer assigned its sole local shadow to array index 0. Other light pools therefore illuminated characters and scenery without casting that lamp's shadow. The fix keeps the accepted eight light sources, color, positions, energy, cone angles and visible beams unchanged.

Two persistent shadow-casting SpotLight slots are reassigned to the strongest fixed source lamps for visible racers and the camera ground center. The other six sources remain ordinary non-shadowed lights. No lamp follows a racer: slot assignments always use an existing fixture's exact source and target. Selection runs only during already-requested night frames, at most every .4 seconds, samples at most 16 visible racers plus the viewport center, and retains a small hysteresis. The two existing shadow textures are reused rather than allocating one per fixture. Actual moving geometry still updates on rendered frames.

Full quality uses at most two local shadow maps: 512x512 in compact/mobile framing, 1024x1024 otherwise. Economy remains zero local shadow maps. PCF radius 1.5 provides a soft edge. The unchanged main directional light remains independent. There is one additional potential local shadow geometry pass versus the former single map; no physical-phone FPS result is claimed. Multiple separated pools in a wide overview are not all simultaneously shadowed: the limited budget prioritizes the viewed actors and central decor.

Shadow strength is physical per-light occlusion. Strong-source occlusion is darker, while other unblocked sources and cool background illumination fill it. Overlap and distance change the depth; no new black decal/silhouette is introduced. Existing full-quality actor bodies and facilities/seating already cast/receive shadows; the existing bounded tree-caster selection remains. Tiny signs/readability overlays and economy shadows are not promoted to full scene casting.

Offline export now carries actual caster flags into GLB extras and the renderer applies them instead of treating every imported mesh as a caster. It also selects shadow sources by the same helper. The controlled before/after character study uses the same models, light energy and camera; only which fixed lights cast shadows differs. Cycles softness, finite cutoff, fill and tone mapping still differ from WebGL. Not an actual gameplay screenshot or mobile rendering acceptance. The historical scene/actor geometry and certificates are untouched.
