## Photometry and offline rendering

Three uses decay 2 and its finite-distance attenuation. The lamp's actual physical panel normal intersects the selected real node's deck plane; the aimed point is not a floating glow. At that receiving point, target illumination is 3.2 scene-linear units and the cutoff distance is 1.65 times the receiving distance. The half cone is π/7 and penumbra .65. All seven map/layout cases are checked for finite intensity, downward physical axis, consistent receiving plane and the declared illuminance.

For Cycles, the offline conversion is P = 4π I × the Three cutoff factor at that receiver. This follows inverse-square point/spot radiant flux rather than treating Three candela as Blender watts. This is a relative scene-linear calibration against the main directional source, **not** measured real-world lux. Cone rolloff, world ambient, shadow filtering, exposure response and tone mapping remain different between engines.

Two 48-sample offline views use the exact runtime environment, raised deck factory, approved pad GLBs, lamp builder and existing cameraFrame/cameraView: Wild 1280×900 and Boardwalks 390×844. They contain no characters, mechanisms, live actions, number textures or HUD. The corresponding PNGs/GLBs/metadata are archived, not represented as browser screenshots. Projection cross-check error is below 2e-5. Actual pixels were viewed: fixtures are supported, warm pools have visible direction/edges, unlit portions remain darker, and neither scene is globally overexposed. The approved pads themselves are dark rubber and are especially dark without the omitted number artwork.

## Budget and checks

Each map submits at most 2,008 new fixture/foundation triangles in eight main-pass batches. Of these, full quality allows 1,960 triangles/six batches to cast shadows. Main plus directional and one local shadow passes give a conservative **20-draw fixture-only** upper bound. This excludes all scenery, actors and routes, whose shadow passes also cost work; it is not the total frame or FPS. Low quality keeps those 2,008 main triangles/eight draws but removes the shadow passes and one local light. It does not pretend zero-scale geometry saves GPU work.
