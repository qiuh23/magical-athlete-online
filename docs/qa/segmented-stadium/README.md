# Segmented stadium seating candidate

The later Mild-only infield/turf review is documented in [infield-review.md](infield-review.md), including its changed budget and new checks. The baseline results below describe the original perimeter candidate.

This is an unpublished local candidate, not a release/contact certificate or a live-device acceptance result.

## Design and ownership

- The U courses use four rear banks plus one return bank. The front and start/finish side remain open. Wild and Whirly have different return-bank positions to clear their actual mechanisms.
- Squiggle and Bridges have three rear terraces and one return terrace. Boardwalks has five rear terraces following its two route shelves plus one return terrace. Natural bases, plants and existing facilities are retained.
- The approved `bleachers.glb`, `spectators.glb`, and `grandstand_rostrum.glb` remain byte-identical. Their full detailed meshes are instanced. New supporting decks, a single side stair per raised bank, railings, nosings and natural end-terrace access tongues are generated by `seating.ts`.
- Infield trees, podium, officials and scoreboard remain. The former central bleachers/crowd/rostrum are replaced, not duplicated.
- No rules, node identities, mechanisms, lighting clocks, cache infrastructure, camera, follow shot or action shot are changed. Every quality tier has identical seat/crowd/structure geometry; economy changes shadows only.

## Offline previews and limits

`export-seating-preview.ts` uses the actual environment builder and real source meshes. Mild follows the actual `DioramaStadium` / `stadium-v7` branch, including its course/base dimensions and start-platform transform. Raised courses use the real mechanism deck/pad builder. The initial prototype that incorrectly used raised dark pads on Mild is superseded.

`render-seating-preview.py` imports the exported geometry in Blender, checks camera projection against the Three camera, saves editable `.blend` scenes and renders PNGs. Canvas labels/grain, actors, rigs, HUD and mechanism hardware are not included. Studio floor, Cycles material/shadow response and tone mapping are presentation approximations. These are explicitly offline scene previews, never game screenshots or FPS/device evidence.

Lighting in the representative comparisons is a coordinated proposal. Its independent final fixture/support collision acceptance remains separate. Before/after images compare the old and new seating with the same proposed lights, not a claim about the old production fixture count.
